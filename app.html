Top Combinations

CombinationTradesW / LWin RateProfitLossNet P/L
Loading…

Scored per strategy — a combo's win rate only reflects trades logged under the Strategy selected in the sticky bar at the top of the page, since the same tags can behave differently across different strategies. Combos of 2 up to 5 confluences (whatever a trade actually carries, capped at 5) need at least 15 trades to be ranked — below that they're folded into an "Other combos" bucket instead of showing a misleadingly clean win rate. Each row is tagged "N-way" so a 3-way combo's edge isn't mistaken for a 2-way pair nested inside it. Only the top 10 qualifying combos (by Net P/L) are shown individually; the rest are folded into "Other qualifying combos."
